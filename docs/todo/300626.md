- Methylation data: cpg common across data platforms

> In our graph, as shown in Fig. [1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12203532/#pgaf177-F1)A, nodes are the CpG sites and edges are their relationships. Following the structure of AltumAge ([27](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12203532/#pgaf177-B27)), we selected CpG sites that were common across all types of methylation data platforms. The structural information for these CpG sites was obtained from the public website NCBI GEO’s [supplementary file](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12203532/#sup1) ([40](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12203532/#pgaf177-B40)). Since the information of distance and position is chromosome specific, CpG site information was prepared based on their chromosomal location, ensuring that their positional and distance information were chromosome-specific.
